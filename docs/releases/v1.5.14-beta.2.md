# Release v1.5.14-beta.2

[![GitHub Downloads](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alexdelprete/ha-abb-fimer-pvi-vsn-rest/v1.5.14-beta.2/total?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/alexdelprete/ha-abb-fimer-pvi-vsn-rest/releases/tag/v1.5.14-beta.2)

**Release Date:** 2026-10-01

**Type:** Beta release — expected outage auto-detect rewritten to learn your plant's night pattern.

## What's Changed

### Changed

- **Auto-detect now learns when your logger switches off and on** (Reported in #79, thanks to piejanssens [@piejanssens](https://github.com/piejanssens)). beta.1 waited for a zero-power reading before the logger went dark, but an inverter that powers a VSN300 keeps feeding tens of watts until the very last poll, so the nightly "Cannot connect" repair still appeared. A dropout is now judged at the last good poll: if the sun was low **and** the plant was producing little, the outage is expected (no repair issue, trigger, recovery script or recovery notification). If the logger is still dark once the sun is above the plant's start-up elevation, it is reported as usual.
- **Learning period.** Every overnight outage is recorded as a night: sun elevation and power when the logger switched off, sun elevation when it came back. For the first 5 nights a built-in starter rule applies (sun below 0° and power below 60 W, start-up by 10°). From then on the bounds are learned from your plant: highest switch-off elevation + 2°, highest switch-off power × 1.5 + 10 W, highest start-up elevation + 2°. The last 14 nights are kept, so the bounds follow the seasons, and a night far off the usual pattern is ignored.
- **Diagnostics** now show the active rule (starter or learned), the learned nights, the bounds and today's resulting window in clock time, under `coordinator.expected_outage`.
- Back-tested on 16 real nights of a VSN300 + PVI-10.0-OUTD: no evening alert and no false morning alert, with the learned rule taking over after night 5.

### Fixed

- **"Cannot connect" repair stuck after a restart during an outage.** The repair is persistent, but the integration only remembered in memory that it had raised it, so after a Home Assistant restart or reload while the logger was dark nothing ever removed it. It is now cleared as soon as setup reaches the datalogger again. This also affected earlier versions.

## Testing Focus

1. Install v1.5.14-beta.2 (HACS, beta versions enabled), restart Home Assistant. Keep **Expected outage handling** on *Auto-detect* (already set if you tested beta.1; the beta.1 calibration data is discarded automatically).
1. Evenings: no "Cannot connect" repair when the inverter shuts down. With the integration's log level at INFO, one line `Datalogger unreachable (... starter rule ...): expected outage, failure notifications deferred`.
1. Mornings: entities come back with no notification, and the log shows `Datalogger back online after ... night recorded; N/5 nights learned ...`.
1. After 5 nights download diagnostics and check `coordinator.expected_outage`: `rule` should be `learned`, with `nights`, the learned bounds and `today_window`. Please paste that block in #79.
1. If a stale "Cannot connect" repair is still listed from beta.1, it disappears at the next successful start.

## Not Affected

- Users who leave the option on **Off** see no change, apart from the stuck-repair fix.
